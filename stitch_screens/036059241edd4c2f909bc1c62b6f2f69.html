COMPETITIONS

Where confidence is won in front of a room.

Annual district-wide platforms in quiz, fine art, and Bengali recitation fostering critical inquiry, cultural memory, and academic courage among school students.

ACADEMIC & CULTURAL CATEGORIES
EVENT CLASS GROUP FORMAT & CRITERIA
Quiz General & Regional Lore Classes 9–10 & 11–12 Teams of 2 pupils per school; written screening prelims followed by 6-team stage finals (General Knowledge, Science, Bengal History & Heritage).
Drawing & Fine Arts Medium: Water & Pastel Classes 6–8 & 9–12 Sit-and-draw on-the-spot thematic contest; art paper supplied by Samiti; themes announced 15 mins prior (Nature, Rural Bengal, Great Reformers).
Bengali Recitation Oratory & Verse Classes 6–8 & 9–12 Solo performance; prescribed classical & modern Bengali verses (Rabindranath Tagore, Kazi Nazrul Islam, Jibanananda Das, Sukanta Bhattacharya).
REGULATORY CODE

Every participant docket must be strictly endorsed with the official round seal and countersignature of the institutional Headmaster or Principal. Private candidates and independent tuitions are non-eligible under statutory bylaws.

Date-of-birth records as per West Bengal Board of Secondary Education (WBBSE) or equivalent central registries must match submitted student declarations precisely. Any discrepancy leads to instant cancellation of standing without appeal.

Late arrivals beyond the final roll call forfeit seat allocation. Electronic communication devices, smartwatches, and preparatory printed reference material are strictly prohibited inside the competition enclosure.

Accompanying teacher mentors will remain seated in the upper gallery of Town Hall and are cordially barred from communicating with candidates once preliminary vetting has commenced.

Evaluation criteria emphasize original interpretation, grammatical fidelity, poise, and conceptual depth. Tabulation sheets are countersigned by the Presiding Registrar and published in the Samiti Gazette.

All district laureates receive commemorative silver plaques, institutional book bursaries worth up to ₹5,000, and automatic nomination to the Medha Chhatravritti state vetting circuit.
