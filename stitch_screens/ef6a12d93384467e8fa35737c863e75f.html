Bardhaman Chhatra Kalyan Samiti

সা বিদ্যা যা বিমুক্তয়ে — Knowledge is that which liberates.

An autonomous, voluntary philanthropic institution dedicated to extending educational endowments, healthcare stipends, and merit-cum-means awards to underprivileged scholars across Purba & Paschim Bardhaman since 2011.

Registration: West Bengal Societies Registration Act XXVI, 1961 (No. S/1L/84931)
Tax Exemption: Certified under Sections 12A & 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
All domestic contributions are eligible for 50% tax deduction under Sec 80G.
