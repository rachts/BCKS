Article XIV

Quorum & Voting Eligibility

WB Societies Act XXVI of 1961

“As stipulated under Section 14 of the Samiti Constitution registered under West Bengal Act XXVI of 1961, one-third of the active registered voting members on the central roll constitutes a valid statutory quorum.”

If no quorum is formed within 30 minutes of the appointed hour (10:30 AM), the assembly stands adjourned to reconvene at the same venue 45 minutes thereafter, where present members shall conduct statutory business and resolve agenda motions irrespective of total registered headcounts, provided no resolution amends the Permanent Corpus Deed.

Statutory Protocol
Order of Conclave

How the AGM is conducted

Parliamentary decorum and transparent archival compliance govern every minute of our general convocation.

1. Stage 09:30–10:15

Registration & Roll Verification

Checking verified photo credentials against the central registrar ledger. Registered voting tokens and printed dossier folios are issued at the entrance counter.

Ledger: General Membership Index
2. Stage 10:30–11:00

Presidential Invocation & Minutes

Reviewing, confirming, and adopting the formal minutes of the preceding annual assembly. Tributes to departed patrons and formal constitutional opening remarks.

Ratification: 14th AGM Gazette
3. Stage 11:00–11:45

Tabling of Secretary’s Report

Detailed presentation of field dispatches, scholarship distributions, textbook banks, and rural outreach programs accomplished during the completed operational year.

Document: Annual Dispatch Dossier
4. Stage 11:45–12:30

Presentation of Audited Ledger

Comprehensive scrutiny by the independent chartered accountant, clarification of balance sheets, and an open floor question-and-answer period for active voting members.

Scrutiny: Sec. 80G Statutory Audit
5. Stage 12:30–01:30

Voting on Resolutions & Endowments

Formal voting via paper slips on operational budgets, corpus preservation terms, appointment of statutory auditors, and policy updates regarding scholarship criteria.

Ballot: Show of Hands / Slips
6. Stage 01:30–02:00

Adjournment & Benediction

Formal recording of adopted resolutions, distribution of printed gazette dossiers, reading of the closing Rabindrasangeet benediction, and formal adjournment sine die.

Conclusion: Minutes Inscribed
