Scholarships

Support that reaches the student who earned it.

Direct financial grants, course study kits, and examination assistance for meritorious students facing severe economic adversity across Purba Bardhaman district.

Annual stipend ceremony, Samiti Parishad Hall. Direct bursaries presented with parental endorsement.

Eligibility Criteria

1

Merit & Financial Hardship

Meritorious student of a government or government-sponsored school, class 9 to 12, currently facing severe documented financial hardship.

2

Institutional Endorsement

Name formally endorsed and sponsored by the Headmaster, Headmistress, or Teacher-in-Charge on official letterhead.

3

Prescribed Submission

Duly completed prescribed physical or digital form submitted within the officially notified calendar window.

4

Personal Verification

Interactive verification interview before the Samiti Selection Board accompanied by at least one custodial parent or legal guardian.

Documentation Required

Please present original copies along with one self-attested duplicate set during interview.

School sponsorship letter with official seal

Signed by Headmaster / Headmistress / TIC stating student conduct and rank.

Previous academic marksheets & admit card

Previous summative examination or Board admit card proving date of birth.

Income certificate / BPL ration documentation

Issued by Municipality Councillor, Panchayat Pradhan, or BDO authority.

Guardian accompanied with proof of identity

Aadhaar or Voter ID card of either mother, father, or legal custodian.
