The days the Samiti comes together.

Gatherings of stewardship, scholastic remembrance, and grassroots solidarity preserved in print and silver gelatin.

Chapter I স্থাপিত ২০১১

Foundation Day প্রতিষ্ঠা দিবস

Each autumn, members, patron teachers, alumni, and scholars gather to commemorate the founding assembly of 2011. An afternoon of musical invocation, annual audit recitals, and public laureation of district merit rankers.

Archival Year:
Chapter II ২৬ সেপ্টেম্বর • স্মরণ ও দান

Birth Anniversary of Pandit Iswarchandra Vidyasagar বিদ্যাসাগর জন্মজয়ন্তী

A solemn tribute to the father of modern Bengali prose and tireless champion of mass education. The Samiti marks 26 September with floral offerings, commemorative lectures by visiting scholars, and free textbook distributions to indigent primary scholars.

Archival Year:
Chapter III অন্যান্য বাৎসরিক অনুষ্ঠান

Other Celebrations & Observances ঋতুভিত্তিক স্মৃতি ও ব্রত

Throughout the academic calendar, the Samiti observes Rabindra Jayanti, Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose's birthday, and annual Saraswati Puja. Each occasion is grounded in grassroots participation, modest community lunches, and shared literary readings.

Archival Year:
Curatorial Archives

Preservation of Public Memory

High-resolution photographic negatives and printed memorial albums are preserved in the Samiti Archives at Chhatra Kalyan Bhavan. Alumni or families seeking reprints of commemorative laureations may write to contact@bardhamanchhatra.org citing the folio reference.
