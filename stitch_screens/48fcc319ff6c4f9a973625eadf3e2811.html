Functions

The days the Samiti comes together.

Foundation Day

প্রতিষ্ঠা দিবস

Each autumn, members, patron teachers, alumni, and scholars gather to commemorate the founding assembly of 2011. An afternoon of musical invocation, annual audit recitals, and public laureation of district merit rankers.

Birth Anniversary of Pandit Iswarchandra Vidyasagar

বিদ্যাসাগর জন্মজয়ন্তী

A solemn tribute to the tireless champion of mass education. The Samiti marks 26 September with floral offerings, commemorative lectures by visiting scholars, and free textbook distributions to primary scholars.

Other Celebrations & Observances

অন্যান্য অনুষ্ঠান

Throughout the year, the Samiti observes Rabindra Jayanti, Netaji's birthday, and annual Saraswati Puja. Each occasion is marked by grassroots participation and shared community meals.
