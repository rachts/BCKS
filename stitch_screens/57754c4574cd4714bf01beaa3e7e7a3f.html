CHAPTER IV • CLAUSE 12 Statutory Privileges

Rights of a Member

  1. 1.
    Attend & vote at the Annual General Meeting (AGM). Direct voice in the allocation of stipends, audit validation, and project ratifications.
  2. 2.
    Stand for election to the Executive Committee. Eligible after one full calendar year of unblemished membership roll standing.
  3. 3.
    Receive audited annual reports & notices. Prompt delivery of the published balance sheet, student grant registries, and secretarial circulars.
CHAPTER IV • CLAUSE 13 Civic Stewardship

Duties & Responsibilities

  1. 1.
    Punctually pay annual dues before 31st March. Enabling seamless planning for the forthcoming secondary school board examinations.
  2. 2.
    Uphold the non-political welfare aims of the Samiti. Preserving strictly secular, partisan-free, and merit-based allocation of community aid.
  3. 3.
    Inform the secretary of any change of address. Ensuring postal delivery of signed certificates, tax forms, and voting ballots.
