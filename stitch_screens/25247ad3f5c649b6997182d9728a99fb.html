Governance & Charter

Annual General Meeting

Under the mandate of the West Bengal Societies Registration Act XXVI of 1961, the sovereign general assembly of patrons, life members, and registered scholars convenes annually to scrutinize the trust’s stewardship, sanction student stipends, and ratify institutional policies.

Statutory Prerequisite

Quorum & Voting Eligibility

In accordance with Clause 14 of the Constitution and the West Bengal Societies Registration Act XXVI of 1961, no business shall be transacted unless a quorum of one-third of registered active members is present in person at the commencement of proceedings. If quorum is not attained within thirty minutes of the scheduled hour, the assembly shall stand adjourned to 11:30 AM on the same date and venue, proceeding thereafter without further minimum quorum requirements on ordinary motions.
