ABOUT US
EST. 2011 • BARDHAMAN, WB

A small organisation with a stubborn belief: money should never end a student’s education.

ARCHIVAL RECORD № 01 CIRC. 2011

Early meeting of founder teachers and community patrons distributing textbooks in Bardhaman town, 2011.

Founded in 2011 by devoted educators and alumni, Bardhaman Chhatra Kalyan Samiti emerged from a quiet realization: every season, brilliant young students across rural and municipal high schools drop out simply for lack of textbook money or school fees.

What began as retired teachers pooling small contributions from their pensions has evolved into an institutional lifeline. We maintain zero corporate overhead — every rupee goes straight to vetted pupils.

CHAPTER I CREED & CHARTER
OUR MISSION verified

"To support deserving students with education, financial assistance, and opportunities."

Ensuring that gifted minds across Bardhaman district have the textbooks, stipends, and mentorship necessary to finish school and build an uncompromised future.

OUR VISION visibility

"A society where financial limitations never prevent a student from realizing their potential."

Fostering self-reliance through unconditional grassroots community support, creating cycles of educational equity across generations.

HISTORICAL CHRONICLE

Years of Service

2011–2026
2011

The Samiti is Formed

Teachers and alumni of Bardhaman Raj Collegiate School establish a mutual welfare corpus with their personal savings to stop secondary dropout.

2013

First Scholarship Awarded

Ten meritorious Madhyamik rankers receive year-long academic stipends, uniform allowances, and complete secondary curricula book collections.

2016

District Quiz Competition

45+ municipal and rural high schools join the annual general knowledge meet, promoting intellectual exchange beyond formal exam curricula.

2018

Health Check-up Camps

Free diagnostic health clinics, pediatric eye exams, corrective spectacles, and iron supplements distributed to over 600 adolescent learners.

2022

Vidyanidhi Scheme Launched

Special multi-year endowment established for bright secondary scholars transitioning into higher undergraduate science, arts, and polytechnic studies.

FOUNDER-SECRETARY & ADVISOR

Sri Baidyanath Singha Roy

A revered teacher and former Assistant Headmaster of Bardhaman Raj Collegiate School, Sri Baidyanath Singha Roy co-founded Students Health Home and spent decades on bicycle visiting indigent families across the district. He continues to guide the Samiti as a permanent Advisory Committee mentor.
