There is a right way for every giver.

An explanation of our welfare funds, memorial endowments, and statutory tax benefits in words — not widgets.

CHAPTER 01 Perpetual Covenant

1. Membership Subscription

Join the covenant of educators and alumni. New membership enrolment contribution is ₹2,000 (one-time lifetime accession to the endowment corpus fund); annual sustaining subscription is ₹500 per year.

Give now →
CHAPTER 02 Permanent In Memoriam

2. Smriti Puraskar

Honour a loved one, teacher, or departed relative through a permanently endowed annual student prize. Endowments of ₹15,000+ support an annual merit medal presented at the Rajbati annual convention.

Give now →
CHAPTER 03 Direct Scholar Ward

3. A Personal Student Sponsorship

Support one identified rural scholar through classes IX to XII. ₹6,000 annually covers books, uniform, coaching, and board registration fees, with verified academic reports twice a year.

“Every ward is paired with a voluntary district preceptor who verifies attendance and ledger expense every academic quarter.”

Give now →
CHAPTER 04 Emergency Solvency

4. The Vidyanidhi Scheme

Emergency term-end relief for students facing family crisis, bereavement, or crop loss, preventing immediate school dropout. Contributions from ₹500 upwards directly replenish this crisis buffer.

Give now →
CHAPTER 05 Statutory Exemption

5. Tax Benefits (80G & 12A)

50% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. Official authenticated receipts and Form 10BE documentation are issued within 48 hours.

Give now →
CHAPTER 06 Institutional Grant

6. Corporate & CSR Giving

We collaborate with registered public and private institutions on Schedule VII CSR educational infrastructure, student science kits, and diagnostic health clinics.
