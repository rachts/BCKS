The hands that run the Samiti.

Statutory Protocols

How we are governed

All council proceedings are recorded in the Samiti Minute Book at the Secretariat. We adhere strictly to the democratic codification laid down in the founding constitution of 1984.

Download Registered By-Laws (PDF 2.4MB) →
How the committee is formed +

Triennial election of 11 office bearers and 6 executive members during the General Body Meeting by secret ballot or unanimous acclamation.

Eligibility mandates at least five consecutive years of uninterrupted active participation in the Samiti’s educational and relief delegations across Purba Bardhaman district.

Tenure of the committee +

A strict three-year term subject to mid-term audited review and ratified annual general reports filed with the Registrar of Societies.

No office bearer may simultaneously hold monetary contracts or receive consultancy disbursements from partner academic institutions during their tenure.

Filling vacancies +

Interim vacancies arising through superannuation or relocation are filled by majority co-option from the registered general council until the next general session.

Co-opted resolutions must obtain statutory quorum confirmation within forty-five calendar days of the formal vacancy announcement.
