CRITERIA OF SELECTION

Who may seek admission to the grant?

1

Government School Enrollment

Meritorious student of a government or government-sponsored institution in Purba Bardhaman, studying in Class 9 to 12, hailing from a financially disadvantaged background.

2

Institutional Endorsement

Candidate’s name and attendance record must be explicitly sponsored by the Headmaster, Headmistress, or Teacher-in-Charge (TIC) on institutional letterhead.

3

Strict Timeline Filing

Application docket must be fully completed and filed with all required annexures strictly within the announced calendar cycle. Late submissions are not entertained.

4

Selection Board Interview

The candidate must appear in person before the Samiti Selection Board accompanied by at least one biological parent or legal guardian, along with all original academic documents.

SCRUTINY DOSSIER

Mandatory documents for interview

Please present original certificates alongside one set of self-attested photocopies.

School Sponsorship Letter Countersigned on official letterhead by current Headmaster, Headmistress, or Teacher-in-Charge.
Official Academic Marksheet Original copy of Madhyamik board scorecard or the most recent school promotion examination report.
Household Income / BPL Certificate Issued by the competent Gram Panchayat Pradhan, Municipality Councillor, or BDO authority.
In-Person Guardian Presence Legal parent or certified guardian must accompany candidate for informal oral confirmation.
