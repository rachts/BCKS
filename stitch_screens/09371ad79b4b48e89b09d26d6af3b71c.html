Discipline Class Cohort

General Knowledge & Heritage Quiz

Cl. 9–10 & 11–12

Two delegates per school team

Modus: Written preliminary screening (50 multiple-choice and archival short queries), followed by a 6-team live stage buzzer finale on literature, regional history, geography, and general science.

Sit-and-Draw Art Assembly

Cl. 6–8 & 9–12

Individual entry • Maximum 3 per institution

Modus: On-the-spot thematic rendition. The Samiti furnishes stamped quarter-imperial cartridge art sheets; theme announced precisely 15 minutes before the commencement of the 90-minute bell.

Bengali Poetry Recitation

Cl. 6–8 & 9–12

Solo declamation • Bengali medium & open

Modus: Solo presentation of classical and modern verses chosen from Rabindranath, Jibanananda, Sukanta, or Shankha Ghosh. Strictly memorized recital capped at three minutes per candidate.
Rules & Protocols Statutory

Open exclusively to bona fide day scholars currently enrolled in recognized government, aided, or private higher secondary institutions across Purba Bardhaman district.

Each official entry form must bear the circular stamp and counter-signature of the Institution Head.

Participating scholars must report in their official school uniform accompanied by a designated teacher escort. Institutional student photo identity cards are mandatory at verification desks.

Electronic apparatus, smart dials, and external printed matter remain strictly barred inside the examination floor.

A neutral panel composed of university professors and literary critics determines all laurels. Decisions delivered by the panel are final and exempt from challenge.

Trophies, parchment citations, and book stipends will be handed during the Sunday Valedictory Session.
