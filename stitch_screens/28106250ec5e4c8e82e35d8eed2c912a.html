Statutory Rules

How we are governed

Enacted under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961. Every post is strictly voluntary without honorarium, allowance, or monetary sitting fee.

Office bearers are elected every two years by the registered General Assembly of life and patron members convened at the Bardhaman Town Hall. No member holding political executive office may stand for election as an active officer.
The elected council serves an un-extended term of twenty-four calendar months. The General Secretary and Treasurer are mandated to publish a notarised public balance sheet thirty days before concluding each biennial tenure.
Casual vacancies arising from resignation, relocation, or demise are filled by consensus nomination through an Extraordinary Working Committee meeting, subject to ratification by the Advisory Council within sixty days.
