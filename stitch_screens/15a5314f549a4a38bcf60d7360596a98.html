01 / PREVENTIVE MEDICAL INITIATIVE

Health Check-up Camps

[PHOTO: HEALTH CAMP]

Volunteer paediatricians and optometrists conducting adolescent vision checkups at Burdwan Municipal High School.

In rural and sub-divisional Purba Bardhaman, chronic undiagnosed vision defects and anemia severely disrupt academic perseverance. Left unchecked, preventable adolescent health impediments quietly compound into sudden classroom dropouts.

Our medical cell acts early, bringing licensed voluntary diagnosticians directly into rural secondary institutions to diagnose, prescribe, and furnish corrective eye gear without cost.

02 / ADOLESCENT GUIDANCE

Counselling & Social Awareness Campaigns

[PHOTO: AWARENESS SESSION]

Interactive career clarity and psychological wellbeing session with secondary scholars at Bardhaman Town Hall.

Academic vulnerability often stems from acute exam anxiety, quiet familial stress, and absolute absence of institutional career navigational advice in first-generation learner households.

Our open dialogic circles de-stigmatize mental wellness, provide confidential listening pods for adolescent anxiety, and empower parents to nurture self-assurance rather than unyielding performance pressure.
