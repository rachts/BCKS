Vision

Key Initiatives

Our Core Activities

01 Flagship Program

Merit-cum-Means Scholarships

Structured stipend awards provided annually to deserving students of Class 9 through 12 across government and state-aided schools in Burdwan. Designed to cover tuition, books, school uniforms, and examination board fees without pause.

Eligibility Guidelines

Who Can Receive a Scholarship

1

Academic Merit & Need

Meritorious student of a government or government-sponsored school, class 9 to 12, from a financially disadvantaged background.

2

Institutional Endorsement

Name sponsored and certified by the Headmaster, Headmistress, or Teacher-in-Charge (TIC) of the attending institution.

3

Formal Application Submission

Duly filled application form submitted within the notified annual window accompanied by family income declaration.

4

Verification Interview

Appear before the Samiti selection board with parent or guardian and all original academic credentials & income documents.
