Membership

Join as a member — stay for the students.

Bardhaman Chhatra Kalyan Samiti thrives on the personal commitment and civic patronage of educators, alumni, and guardians of Purba Bardhaman. Membership is an active covenant to sustain grassroots secondary scholarships, diagnostic health camps, and cultural literacy assemblies across our district.

Rights of a General Member

  1. 1.

    Attend and exercise voting franchise at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) and special convocations.

  2. 2.

    Stand for election and nominate peers to the Governing Body and Executive Committee.

  3. 3.

    Receive audited annual financial gazettes, programmatic reports, and official notices by post and digital dispatch.

  4. 4.

    Propose amendments to welfare policies and inspect the registered Samiti rolls during designated secretariat hours.

Duties & Civic Responsibilities

  1. 1.

    Pay the prescribed annual membership subscription punctually before the closure of the audit cycle (31st March).

  2. 2.

    Uphold the non-political, charitable ethos and educational aims of the Samiti across Purba Bardhaman institutions.

  3. 3.

    Promptly notify the General Secretary in writing of any change in postal address, contact numbers, or institutional affiliation.

  4. 4.

    Participate actively in volunteer delegations during annual scholarship verification drives and public health clinics.

Procedure for Enrolment

Three simple steps to formal membership

1

Fill the form

Complete the simple biographical dossier online or download the bilingual physical form to record your residency in Purba Bardhaman.

2

Pay by UPI or bank transfer

Remit the membership fee via direct Aadhaar/UPI transfer to the registered State Bank account or submit at the Curzon Gate central desk.

3

Receive your membership receipt by email

Obtain your official digitally authenticated 80G tax receipt, membership register number, and voting voucher within 48 hours.
