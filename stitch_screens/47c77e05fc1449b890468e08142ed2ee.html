WAYS TO GIVE PURBA BARDHAMAN // PHILANTHROPIC PROTOCOLS // ESTD. 2011

There is a right way for every giver.

Bardhaman Chhatra Kalyan Samiti operates purely on civic solidarity, local educator patronage, and community endowments. We do not solicit impulsive donations or employ commercial campaign tools. Below are the statutory, time-honoured pathways through which citizens, teachers, and alumni sustain students across Purba Bardhaman.

1. Membership Subscription

CONSTITUTIONAL ENROLMENT

Joining the Samiti is both a governance voice and an enduring contribution. New membership enrolment contribution is ₹2,000 (one-time lifetime accession towards the students' corpus fund), and annual sustaining renewal is ₹500 per year. Members receive voting rights at the Annual General Meeting, eligibility for the governing council, and dispatch of the printed financial gazette and auditor's balance sheet each winter.

Give now → ANNUAL RENEWAL DUE: 31 MARCH

2. Smriti Puraskar (Memorial Endowment)

PERPETUAL ENDOWMENT

Honour a departed teacher, elder, or loved one through a permanently endowed annual student prize or medal. An endowment of ₹15,000 or more establishes an annual prize conferred during the annual convention in their name to a top-performing secondary student from rural schools in Purba Bardhaman. The capital remains untouched in scheduled treasury deposits; only annual interest yields are disbursed as citation rolls and scholastic assistance.

Give now → CORPUS BOND PRESERVED IN PERPETUITY

3. A Personal Student Sponsorship

DIRECT SCHOLASTIC CARE

Support one named boy or girl through a crucial academic year (Classes IX to XII). A recurring contribution of ₹6,000 annually covers textbooks, school uniforms, board examination fees, and localized subject coaching, with progress reports delivered to you twice a year under the seal of the Headmaster's verification. Sponsors are welcome to correspond with student recipients through the Secretariat desk.

Give now → BI-ANNUAL ACADEMIC AUDIT PROVIDED

4. The Vidyanidhi Scheme

CONTINGENCY EMERGENCY POOL

A dedicated emergency buffer for enrolled students facing sudden domestic bereavement, acute medical crises, or crop failure affecting their household. Vidyanidhi stipends prevent abrupt school dropouts before the Madhyamik and Higher Secondary examinations. Donations of any amount from ₹500 upwards directly bolster this relief pool, accessible within 24 hours upon field verification by our rural circle inspectors.

Give now → DISBURSED UNDER HEADMASTER WARRANT

5. Statutory Tax Benefits (Section 80G & 12A)

REVENUE RECOGNITION

Bardhaman Chhatra Kalyan Samiti is registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act XXVI of 1961. Contributions enjoy 50% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (Order No. CIT(E)/KOL/80G/2021-22). Form 10BE certificates are furnished directly via digital dispatch within 48 hours and registered on the IT portal before each annual assessment deadline, ensuring seamless pre-fill on your annual tax return.

Give now → Download 80G Exemption Certificate (PDF) ↓

6. Corporate & Institutional CSR Giving

SCHEDULE VII MANDATES

We collaborate with accredited public sector units, banks, and corporate entities on audited educational infrastructure, student science kits, and diagnostic health camps under Schedule VII CSR guidelines. Formal compliance reports, photographic records, and third-party utility auditing certificates are presented to corporate CSR boards with statutory precision.
