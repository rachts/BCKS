Bardhaman Chhatra Kalyan Samiti is a grassroots educational trust driven by retired teachers, civil servants, and alumni. Since 2011, we have stood beside impoverished students across Purba Bardhaman district with stipends, textbooks, and moral guidance.

Our Creed & Foundation

Our Mission

To identify, adopt, and support deserving students with education, financial assistance, and opportunities, ensuring socio-economic distress never truncates an earnest academic journey.

Our Vision

To create an equitable society where financial limitations never prevent a promising young mind from realizing their true moral, academic, and professional potential.

◆ Transparency & Process

Who Can Receive A Scholarship?

Beneficiaries are vetted rigorously through a 4-tier grassroots verification framework to ensure aid reaches the most genuine students.

01

Classes 9 to 12 in Govt/Aided Schools

Regular bona fide students in Purba Bardhaman whose family annual income falls below the rural poverty threshold.

02

Official School Endorsement

Application must be formally countersigned by the Headmaster, Headmistress, or Teacher-in-Charge confirming merit & conduct.

03

Duly Completed Prescribed Form

Attached marksheets, ration card/BPL certificate copy, and handwritten declaration by the candidate.

04

Direct Guardian Interview

In-person session accompanied by a parent or legal guardian alongside original academic scorecards.
